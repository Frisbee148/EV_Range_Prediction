### Notes / gaps

- **2020 Tesla Model 3** zip is incomplete (`.part` only) — excluded.
- **2023 Ioniq 5** Analysis.tdms has V/I (Hioki-like Udc1/Idc1) but **no SOC channel**.
- **D3 grade is ~0%** (chassis dyno). Elevation term is dead for D3 — use TUM/MoST for grade.
- **Range labels (range_a/b)** are not fabricated from dyno traces; they require the §6 physics + E14 pipeline.
- Static vehicle parameters left NaN here on purpose — fill from EPA (`data/raw/epa/`) / `configs/vehicles.yaml`.
